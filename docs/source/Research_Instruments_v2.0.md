## 1.1 Consent and Briefing

*Screen 1 of the online instrument set. Presented on Day 1 before any teaching content.*

### Research Participation: Information and Consent

**Doctoral research study, SDA Bocconi School of Management**

Thank you for taking part in this training programme.

The training materials used in this programme were designed with the assistance of artificial intelligence, as part of a doctoral research project at SDA Bocconi School of Management, supervised by Professor Emanuele Borgonovo.

**About the research**

This study examines how AI-assisted design affects the quality of finance training for working professionals, how people experience gamified simulation, and what makes such training effective and trustworthy.

**What you are being asked to do**

During this four-day programme you may complete four short activities: a brief questionnaire at the start of Day 1, a short reflection at the end of each day (about five minutes), and a final evaluation at the end of Day 4 (about ten minutes).

**Your responses are anonymous**

- No name, employee number, email address, or any other identifying information is collected at any point
- You do not log in, and these forms are completely separate from the FinPlay training platform
- Your responses cannot be linked to you, to each other, or to anything you do in the training
- Your employer will never see individual responses, because no individual responses exist to see

**Your choice is free**

Taking part in the research is entirely voluntary and separate from your attendance at the training, which your employer arranged. Choosing not to take part has no effect whatsoever on your training experience and no employment consequence of any kind.

You may stop at any time simply by not completing a later activity. Because responses carry no identifiers, a submitted response cannot afterwards be found and withdrawn.

**Questions**

Contact the researcher, Aiman S. Sadeq, or the institutional ethics contact at ethics@unibocconi.it.

# PART 3: FACILITATOR RUN SHEET

*For the facilitator only. Not shown to participants.*

The instruments are self-administered online. The facilitator's role is to read the briefing, display the access link, and then step back. **Do not circulate, do not look at screens, and do not assist with completion.** If asked a question about an item, answer only about what the item means, never about what to write.

## Day 1, before any teaching content

**Step 1.** Read the following aloud, in English and Arabic:

> "Before we begin, I want to tell you about something. The training materials you will use this week were designed with artificial intelligence, as part of my doctoral research at SDA Bocconi School of Management, supervised by Professor Emanuele Borgonovo.
>
> You are invited to take part in that research by completing a few short feedback activities during the programme. This is completely voluntary and entirely separate from the training itself, which your employer arranged. Whether you take part or not makes no difference to your training and has no work consequence at all.
>
> Everything is anonymous. There is no name, no employee number, no login. These forms are completely separate from the FinPlay platform, so nothing you write can be connected to you or to anything you do in the training. I will never know who wrote what, and neither will your employer.
>
> The link is on the screen. The first page explains everything I have just said, and asks whether you would like to take part. Please choose whichever answer is right for you."

**Step 2.** Display the link and QR code. Allow about three minutes. **Everyone opens the link, whether taking part or not.**

**Step 3.** Display the pre-training questionnaire link to the whole room. Allow about five minutes. Do not check who is completing it.

## End of Days 1, 2, 3 and 4

Display the daily reflection link before people leave. Allow about five minutes.

> "Before you go, if you are taking part in the research, please take five minutes for today's reflection. Your honest views are what make this useful."

## End of Day 4, after the reflection

Display the post-training evaluation link. Allow about ten minutes.

> "This is the last one, and it is a little longer, about ten minutes. Detailed answers here are the most useful thing you can give me."

## After each session

- Confirm submission counts per instrument in the admin view. **Counts only, never contents, during the programme.**
- Record anything unusual in the deviations log: technical failures, fallback to paper, interruptions, anything said in the room that might have influenced responses.
- Write the reflexivity journal entry the same evening.

## If technology fails

Printed copies of every instrument travel to each session. If the network fails, devices are unavailable, or the client network blocks access, revert to paper for the affected instrument: participants complete it unaided, place it in the collection box unfolded and unmarked, and the box is sealed in the room and carried by the client coordinator. **Record the substitution in the deviations log.**

# PART 4: CHANGES FROM VERSION 1.0

| # | Change | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Name, date and signature fields removed from the consent form** | v1.0 collected a signed identifier, which contradicts anonymity at source and would have created exactly the personal data the PDPL position depends on not existing. Consent is now given by choosing one of two options on screen |
| 2 | **Both consent options presented with equal prominence, and both continue to the same confirmation screen** | Makes declining invisible in the room. v1.0 required returning a signed form to the facilitator |
| 3 | **Job title, department and free-text years of experience replaced with coarse bands** | Job title plus department is close to a unique identifier in a cohort of about 25. Role band and experience band carry the analytic value without the identification risk. Department dropped entirely |
| 4 | **"Prefer not to say" added to both demographic items** | Costs nothing and removes any felt pressure to self-describe |
| 5 | **Programme length corrected from three days to four throughout** | v1.0 was written for the earlier three-day ministry format. Cohort 1 runs four days |
| 6 | **Fourth daily reflection added, and Day 4 gains a cross-programme question** | Matches the four-day format and Protocol v1.1 section 4 |
| 7 | **Facilitator instruction to give the questionnaire "only to those who consented" removed** | That step made declining visible to the facilitator. All links are now displayed to the whole room |
| 8 | **Anonymity explained concretely in the consent text** | No login, separate from FinPlay, no linkage between responses. Under online collection participants cannot see anonymity being protected, so it has to be described precisely rather than asserted |
| 9 | **Voluntariness reminder added to the head of each later instrument** | Withdrawal by non-completion only works if people are reminded it remains available |
| 10 | **Paper fallback procedure added to the run sheet** | Removes single-point-of-failure risk from a collection that cannot be repeated |
| 11 | **Sealed-envelope return retained for the paper fallback only** | Correct procedure when paper is actually used |
