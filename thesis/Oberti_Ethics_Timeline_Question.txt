MY SITUATION, BRIEFLY

Fieldwork begins on 6 September, twelve days from now. Three cohorts of working
professionals run inside ten days: one four-day programme from 6 to 9 September, and two
three-day programmes on 6 to 8 and 13 to 15 September. These are commercial training
deliveries that will run whatever happens; what is at stake is only whether I may collect
research data alongside them.

The collection is modest. Four short forms on participants' own phones: a brief
questionnaire on day one, a two-minute reflection at the end of each day, and an
evaluation on the final day. They are anonymous at source. No name, no employee number,
no email address, no login, and no linkage between one submission and another. They are
administered on a platform separate from the training software, so nothing a participant
writes can be connected to anything they do in the course. Participants are adults, the
subject matter is professional finance training, and no special categories of data are
involved.

WHAT I WOULD LIKE TO KNOW

  1. Is approval within twelve days realistic, or is that outside the committee's normal
     rhythm? I would rather plan around the real answer than hope.

  2. If it is not realistic, what are my options? Specifically, is data collected before
     approval simply unusable, or is there a route by which a cohort can be brought into
     the study afterwards? I ask because these three cohorts are not repeatable: there is
     no fourth session in this window, and the next comparable opportunity is some months
     away.

  3. Is there anything in an application of this profile that typically causes delay, so
     that I can address it before submitting rather than in a round of queries?

I will start the application today regardless of the answer, since I understand the
