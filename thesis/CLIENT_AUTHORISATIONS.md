Covers the cohort running 6 to 9 September, four days, self-delivered.

**What it is.** Gatekeeper permission to approach the organisation's staff, given by the
person responsible for the programme, after a description of what the research involves.

**What it is not.** It is not consent. Every participant still decides individually, and
declining has to remain procedurally invisible. An employer's blessing does not travel
down to the individual, and the protocol treats the two as separate throughout.

**And it raises a risk rather than removing one.** The likeliest failure in the room is a
manager telling people to fill the forms in, and an organisation that has formally agreed
to the research makes that more likely, not less. Point 3 of the facilitator briefing
exists for this. If the room is instructed, the consent is not consent and no data from
that cohort can be used.
