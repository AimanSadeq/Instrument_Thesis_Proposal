Good news on NUPCO. Two cohorts confirmed, 6 to 8 September and 13 to 15 September, both
delivered by Moayad.

There is a research element I need to put in front of you before you see them next week.
FinPlay is the subject of my doctorate at SDA Bocconi, and I collect anonymous feedback from
participants in the programmes we run. I would like NUPCO to be part of that. It changes
nothing commercial and adds nothing to what we deliver, but it needs one thing from the
client, and it has to be asked for in advance rather than on the day.


WHAT I NEED FROM THEM

One written confirmation from their side, from whoever owns the training relationship, ideally
copying whoever owns their data policy: that their people may complete a short anonymous
research questionnaire on their own phones, on forms hosted outside the NUPCO network.

That is the whole ask. A reply saying yes is enough. I need it before 6 September for the
first cohort.


WHAT IT INVOLVES FOR THEIR PEOPLE

Everything is on their own phones and takes two to five minutes each time. A consent screen on
the morning of Day 1, a short questionnaire after the opening briefing, a brief reflection at
the end of each day, and an evaluation at the end of Day 3.


WHAT WE CAN PROMISE THEM

No names, no employee numbers, no email addresses, no login. Nothing that identifies a person
is collected anywhere, so nothing can be traced back to anyone afterwards, by us or by them.

Taking part is each person's own decision, and declining is invisible. The screen presents
taking part and not taking part the same way, and both are submitted with the same tap, so
Moayad cannot tell who did which and neither can NUPCO.

The forms are hosted in the United States. Say so plainly if it comes up, because it is the
question their data people will ask. No personal data is collected, so none of theirs leaves
the Kingdom, but they are entitled to know where the forms sit and some organisations have a
rule about it. That is precisely why I want this confirmed in writing beforehand rather than
discovered on Day 1.


WHAT WE CANNOT GIVE THEM

Individual responses, ever, and no list of who took part. Neither exists. If they want
something back, what we can offer is a summary at group level once both cohorts are finished.
Please do not offer more than that.


ONE THING TO AVOID

Attendance at the training is theirs to arrange. Participation in the research is not. It must
not be presented to their staff as part of the course or as something expected of them. If a
manager tells the room to complete it, the consent stops being consent and I cannot use any of
the data.

Moayad will have the full briefing and a run sheet before the first cohort, so none of this
falls to you or to them to explain. It is scripted.

  - The name, role and email of whoever at NUPCO owns the training relationship, and if you
    know it, who owns their data policy. I have the request drafted and ready to send, but I
    will not send it to a generic address.

  - Whether you would rather I send that request or you do. It is written over my signature,

If NUPCO push back on any part of it, tell me rather than working around it. There is usually
