PART B: Year 2 Literature Review Plan
The literature review serves two purposes: a focused ~3,000–3,500-word review for the TP (Year 2), and a comprehensive ~18,000–22,000-word review for the final thesis (Year 3). This plan covers both, organised by the seven literature streams established in the literature map.
Logical Structure: How the 7 Streams Connect to the 5 RQs
The literature review follows a funnel structure: it opens with the broadest context (AI reshaping instructional design), narrows through the specific design sciences (multimedia learning, gamification), grounds the work in the domain (adult financial education), introduces the evaluation and adoption lenses, and closes by establishing the convergence gap that justifies the study. The final sub-section (2.7) presents the integrative conceptual framework.

Narrative Flow for the TP Literature Review (~3,000–3,500 words)
The TP literature review is not a mini-thesis LR. It is a focused argument that establishes the gap and justifies the research design. The recommended flow:
Open with AI’s impact on instructional design (Stream 1, ~450 words). Establish that AIEd research overwhelmingly targets students, not instructional designers or corporate trainers. Cite Zawacki-Richter et al. (2019), Yan et al. (2024), Muljana et al. (2024). Introduce the UNESCO governance framework.
Present the evidence-based design sciences that should underpin any AI-assisted design (Streams 2–3, ~900 words). Mayer’s 15 principles, Clark & Mayer’s eLearning guidelines, Gagné’s events. Then gamification: SDT, MDA, meta-analytic evidence (Sailer & Homner, calibrated claims). Note: Ponce et al. (2024) is the only study applying multimedia principles to financial tasks.
Ground in the domain (Stream 4, ~400 words). Adult learning theory (Knowles), financial education effectiveness (Kaiser et al., 2022 — the debate is resolved), OECD/INFE competency architecture as the outcome vocabulary.
Introduce the evaluation and adoption lenses (Streams 5–6, ~700 words). Kirkpatrick NWKM with critiques (Holton, Alliger & Janak), then TAM/UTAUT lineage culminating in Venkatesh (2022) AI-specific agenda. Both streams show no application to AI-enhanced corporate training.
Synthesise into the conceptual framework (~400 words + diagram). Show how the six theoretical lenses map to the five RQs. State the convergence gap explicitly: zero published studies at the intersection of AI-assisted instructional design + gamification + finance professional training + DBR methodology. This is the “punch line” of the LR.
Stream-by-Stream Narrative Structure (for the full thesis LR)
For each of the seven streams, the full thesis literature review should follow this internal logic:
Historical foundations & seminal works — establish the theoretical bedrock
Recent developments & empirical evidence — show the current state of knowledge
Critical assessment — identify limitations, debates, and contested findings
Application to this study’s context — finance professional training, GCC/MENA region
Specific gap this thesis addresses — explicitly stated at the end of each stream section
