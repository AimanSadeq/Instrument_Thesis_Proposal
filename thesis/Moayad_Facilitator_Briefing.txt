Two NUPCO cohorts, 6 to 8 and 13 to 15 September, both yours. Everything about the

WHAT IT ACTUALLY IS

Four short forms on participants' own phones. A consent screen on the morning of Day 1,
a questionnaire after the opening briefing, a two-minute reflection at the end of each
day, and an evaluation at the end of Day 3. You display a link and a QR code at each
point and then step back. That is the whole of your involvement.

The forms are anonymous. No name, no employee number, no login. You cannot see what
anyone wrote, and neither can I until after the cohort finishes. The admin page shows
you counts and nothing else, and that is not a setting I can change for you: the secret
you hold has no route to the contents.

FOUR THINGS THAT MATTER MORE THAN THEY LOOK

  1. Do not help anyone complete a form. Answer what a question means if asked. Never
     what to write.

  2. Do not look at anyone's screen, and do not walk around while they are completing.

  3. If a manager tells the room to fill it in, stop it, politely, there and then.
     Participation has to be each person's own choice. If the room is instructed, the
     consent is not consent and I cannot use any of the data from that cohort.

  4. Counts only during the programme, never contents.

Those four are not house style. They are commitments in writing to the university, and
the study fails its ethics test without them.

ONE CHANGE TO THE SCRIPT, AND IT MATTERS

The Day 1 briefing script is written for me to read. Where it says "my doctoral
research", you say "the doctoral research of my colleague Aiman Sadeq". Do not say it
is yours. It is the moment people decide whether to take part, and a wrong word there
is the worst place to have one. I have updated the run sheet so the substitution is
printed next to the script; you will not have to remember it.

The run sheet is also written for four days. Yours is three. Read every "Day 4" as your
last day. The screens handle this themselves and will show the right days; only the
paper version needs you to notice.

WHAT I NEED YOU TO WRITE DOWN

Whenever you depart from the materials, note it the same day. One line is plenty.
"Skipped the icebreaker, group too senior." "Case study in Workshop 3 assumed they knew
working capital, they did not, drew it on the board." "Round 1 took 35 minutes not 20."

I am asking because it is the most valuable thing in the whole exercise for me. The
materials were designed with AI, and my research question is what a human expert has to
change when those materials meet a real room. You are the only person who can see that,
because you did not design them and I did. Three days of scribbles beat an hour of
recall two weeks later.
