WHAT WE ARE ASKING OF YOUR PEOPLE

Four short forms, completed on their own phones, two to five minutes each: a consent
screen on the morning of Day 1, a brief questionnaire after the opening briefing, a
short reflection at the end of each day, and an evaluation at the end of Day 3.
Participation is each person's own decision and is entirely separate from attending
the training.


WHAT IS AND IS NOT COLLECTED

Nothing that identifies a person is collected at any point. Specifically, and in the
terms your data team will want:

  - No name, employee number, email address or job title. Role and experience are
    recorded only as broad bands.
  - No login and no account. There is no sign-in screen, so no participant list and
    no contact record is created at any stage.
  - No cookie, no browser storage, no device identifier.
  - No IP address and no browser user-agent is retained.
  - No time of day. Only the training day and the calendar date.
  - No link between any two responses, including between one person's Day 1 and
    Day 3 answers, and no connection of any kind to the FinPlay training platform
    your people log in to.

Because no personal data is collected, there is no individual record to disclose,
correct or delete afterwards, and we say so plainly to participants rather than
promising a withdrawal we could not perform.


HOSTING, STATED PLAINLY

The forms are hosted in the United States, on Render and Supabase. I would rather
put that in front of you now than have it surface later. Since no personal data
enters the dataset, this involves no cross-border transfer of personal data within
the meaning of the Personal Data Protection Law. Connection metadata such as IP
addresses is handled transiently at the network layer by the hosting provider, as it
is for any online form including the common hosted survey tools, and is not logged,
not retained and not associated with any response.
