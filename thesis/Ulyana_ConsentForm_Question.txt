The reason I cannot judge question 1 myself is that my first cycle has two kinds of

The first is anonymous at source. Participants complete short questionnaires on their

person; and no IP address or time of day is retained. There is no personal data in
the dataset to protect, because none is ever collected.

The second is two semi-structured interviews with named practitioners, under written
individual consent. An interview cannot be anonymous to the interviewer, so those two
participants are identifiable to me and are offered confidentiality rather than
anonymity.

Had the study been only the first kind, I would have judged that the institutional
process could reasonably accompany the larger phase. The two interviews are what make
me unwilling to decide that on my own.

Fieldwork starts on 6 September, so if the Consent Form is required beforehand I would
