Authorship & AI protocol

how the document stays yours — and provably so

AI sits in this thesis at two levels, and the TP must keep them cleanly separated: AI as the research object (the AI-designed training artefacts, with prompt/output logs already part of your governance protocol) and AI as production assistance for the TP document itself. The school requires disclosure of the second; your methodology already showcases the first.

  1. You seed every section before AI touches it. Bullets, a voice memo, a rough paragraph — the argument enters in your words. AI structures, tightens, and checks; it does not originate positions.
  2. Three passages are yours alone: the positionality statement (3.4), every interpretive claim in Preliminary Findings (5.2), and the final formulation of the research gap. These carry your judgement — the thing being examined.
  3. You rewrite what carries the argument. Topic sentences, transitions, and all evaluative language get a rewrite pass in your own phrasing. The read-aloud test in Week 5 is the gate: anything that doesn’t sound like you goes back.
  4. Keep the version trail. Your seed notes, AI-assisted intermediates, and your rewrites, dated. This is the audit trail that makes the disclosure statement verifiably true — and it mirrors the prompt/output logging your own methodology applies to the training artefacts.
  5. No AI-fabricated scholarship. Every reference comes from your verified map or a logged search; AI formats citations but never introduces a source you haven’t seen.
Draft disclosure skeleton (per the school’s own template, finalised once your AI-ethics document arrives): “During production of this work, the author utilized generative artificial intelligence, namely [tools]. These tools were used to structure drafts from the author’s outlines and notes, format references, check consistency, and copy-edit; all research questions, study design, interpretations, and conclusions are the author’s own, and AI-generated training materials are themselves an object of study described in the Methodology. The author reviewed all content and asserts the content within this document is factually accurate and free of plagiarism. The author takes full responsibility for the submitted document.”
