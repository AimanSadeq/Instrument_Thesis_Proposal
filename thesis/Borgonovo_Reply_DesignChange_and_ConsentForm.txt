WHAT CHANGED

When I wrote to you on 21 August, Cycle 1 was one cohort in September, delivered by
me, with no identifiable participants anywhere. Three things have moved since.

  - A second client confirmed two further cohorts, 6 to 8 and 13 to 15 September.
    They run three days rather than four, and a colleague of mine delivers both. I
    am neither present nor observing. Their participation in the research still
    depends on that client's written authorisation, which I have requested and not
    yet received, so I am writing the proposal so that nothing depends on it.

  - Because two cohorts are delivered by someone who did not design the materials,
    Cycle 1 now observes the same programme with and without the designer in the
    room. I have written that up carefully rather than enthusiastically: it is
    confounded by organisation, sector and programme length all at once, so I treat
    it as a signal worth examining and not as a controlled comparison.

  - I have added two interviews to Cycle 1, one with the colleague who delivers and
    one with a client training contact. They exist because the learner instruments
    cannot reach two of the three things the redesign is supposed to refine: how the
    design work was divided between the AI and me, and what governs whether an
    organisation adopts materials of this kind. Learners see neither.

THE POINT THAT MATTERS FOR THE CONSENT FORM

That third change is the one that alters the question. Until now every participant
was anonymous at source: no identifier collected anywhere, no linkage between one
response and another, nothing that could be traced to a person. That is the basis on
which I judged the institutional process could reasonably wait for Cycle 2.

An interview cannot be anonymous to the interviewer. Those two participants are known
to me by name. What they are offered is confidentiality, which is a weaker promise,
and I say so in those terms in the protocol rather than blurring it. So the study now
has identifiable human participants in Cycle 1, and I no longer think I should be the
one deciding whether that can proceed on your review alone.

My question to Ulyana and Giada is therefore this, and it is different from what I
sent you:

    A doctoral study with two strands of data in its first cycle. The first is
    anonymous at source, with no identifier collected at any point and no linkage
    between responses. The second is two semi-structured interviews with named
    practitioners, under written individual consent. Fieldwork begins 6 September
    2026. Does the Consent Form process at ecr.unibocconi.it need to be completed
    before that date, or may it accompany the larger multi-organisation cycle that
    follows? And if it is required now, what does the Consent Form consist of: a
    template we give participants, or an application for committee review?

If it must be filed before 6 September, I would rather know this week than next.
