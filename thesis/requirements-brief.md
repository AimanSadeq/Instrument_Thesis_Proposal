# SDA Bocconi DBA Thesis Proposal (TP) — Requirements Brief
Source: DBA_8_Guidelines_for_Candidates.pdf (§3.2, 3.4, 3.5, 3.6) + TP_Cover_Page_DBA.doc

## What the TP is
- Year-2 deliverable. Develops around the research question(s) already formulated in Year 1 (LR year), "speaks back" to the literature.
- Must cover: research setting, methodology (quant and/or qual), preliminary empirical findings.
- Detailed empirical findings (data collection + analysis) NOT compulsory at TP stage — include only if data already gathered and supervisor deems appropriate.
- Early step: a brief no-word-count summary document (project summary incl. LR + RQs) to supervisor in first couple of months of Year 2, as basis of discussion. Ongoing supervisor iteration on drafts is expected; final version must be supervisor-approved BEFORE submission.

## Required structure (exact)
1. Cover Page (template from Blackboard = the .doc provided)
2. Introduction of the Research Project, Literature Review and Research Question(s) — overview of RQ, extant literature, gap addressed
3. Empirical setting — industry/company/occupation; geographical area; research sample; how/when candidate accessed the setting; rationale for choice; candidate's familiarity with setting/participants
4. Methodology — data collection methods (surveys, interviews…) and data analysis (grounded theory, thematic, statistical…) whenever available
5. Empirical findings (if any) — preliminary description + interpretation
6. List of references
7. Appendices — tables, figures, interview protocols, experimental protocols, survey instruments, other material

## Length & submission
- 8,000–11,000 words EXCLUDING references and appendices.
- Final PDF uploaded to Blackboard by 30 September of Year 2. (One-year grace to 30 Sept Year 3; missing that = dismissal.)
- Qualitative grade (e.g. "Very Good") by end of November.

## Evaluation criteria (the 4 things graded)
1. Conceptual framework clearly outlined
2. Research design transparently and thoroughly described
3. Research design appropriate for the study
4. Advantages AND limitations of the research design clearly discussed

## Formatting (§3.4)
- A4; font one of Arial/Tahoma/Times New Roman/Verdana; 12pt; DOUBLE spacing; 2.5cm margins all sides.
- Cover page: same font as body; must include title, candidate name, supervisor name, date, and EXACT word count (word count = including references, excluding tables/figures/appendices). NB: cover word count includes refs; the 8–11k band excludes refs — two different counts.
- Table of contents with page numbers for all chapters/sub-chapters.
- **Statement regarding the use of AI (REQUIRED front-matter section)** — disclose gen-AI tools used. Template wording given by school:
  "During production of this work, the author(s) utilized generative artificial intelligence, namely [LIST TOOLS]. These tools were used to [DESCRIBE WHERE, HOW, AND WHY]. The author(s) reviewed all content and assert the content within this document is factually accurate and free of plagiarism. The author(s) take full responsibility for the submitted document."
- References: APA style, consistent (Mendeley etc. allowed). In-text citations APA: (Author, year), (Author & Author, year), (Author et al., year); direct quotes add p. number.
- No endnotes; footnotes discouraged.
- Questionnaires/interview guides/instruments MUST be included — appendix for instruments, main body for figures/charts.
- Page numbers: Roman (i, ii…) for dedication/abstract/acknowledgements/ToC/AI statement; Arabic for the rest; cover excluded.

## Ethics (§3.5)
- BEFORE starting empirical inquiry: discuss SDA Bocconi ethics procedures with supervisor.
- Human-subjects research (interviews, experiments, surveys) → must complete "Consent Form" via ecr.unibocconi.it (Bocconi Ethics Committee portal). Questions: ethics@unibocconi.it.

## Context for the final thesis (§3.3, later target, informs TP choices)
- FT structure: Cover, Dedication*, Abstract (350w), Acknowledgments*, ToC, Lists, Introduction, Literature Review, Research Setting, Methodology, Empirical Findings, Discussion, Conclusions, References, Appendices (*optional).
- FT evaluated on: clear RQ/domain understanding; empirical component transparent/justified/rigorous; original contribution to research; original contribution to practice. Managerial implications must be prominent (DBA = practitioner degree).
- Theory may be deductive or inductive but must be present.
- Typical length 70–100 pages (not formal requirement). Defense: 30-min PPT + 10-min Q&A, January.
