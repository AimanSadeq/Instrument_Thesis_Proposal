## Statement of the research being undertaken

This doctoral research at SDA Bocconi School of Management examines how generative
artificial intelligence can be used, under expert human review, to design corporate
finance training, and how well the resulting program works in practice. The training
itself, a finance program for non-finance professionals built around a team-based
business simulation, is delivered as a normal part of participants' professional
development. The research collects feedback on the quality of the materials, engagement
with the program, and learning outcomes. There is no hidden purpose: the study is fully
described in advance, and no deception is involved.

## Procedures and duration

LEARNERS: participation consists of up to four short activities completed on your own
phone or device during the training program: a brief questionnaire at the start of Day 1
(about 5 minutes), a short reflection at the end of each training day (about 2 minutes
each), and an evaluation on the final day (about 10 minutes). Total time across the
program is roughly 20 to 25 minutes. The forms are hosted on a platform separate from
the training software, and completing them requires no account and no login.

PRACTITIONERS: two professionals involved in delivering or commissioning the program are
invited to one semi-structured interview each, of approximately 45 minutes, about how the
AI-designed materials performed in live delivery.

## Expected benefits and foreseeable risks

There is no direct benefit to participants beyond the training program itself, which is
provided in full whether or not anyone takes part in the research. The wider benefit is
to the design of professional finance education: participant feedback directly informs
how AI-assisted training materials are evaluated and improved. There are no foreseeable
risks beyond those ordinarily encountered in daily life.

## Voluntary Participation

Participation is voluntary. You may skip any question, stop at any time, and withdraw
without penalty and without any effect on your training, your assessment, or your
standing with your employer. For learners, declining is designed to be invisible: the
forms are completed privately on your own device, taking part and not taking part look
the same to everyone in the room, and neither the facilitator nor your employer can see
who participated. Attendance at the training is a matter for your organization;
participation in the research is yours alone.

## Compensation

There is no compensation for taking part. The training program is provided by your
organization independently of the research.

## Deception

Not applicable. The study involves no deception; its purpose is fully described before
any data is collected.

## Confidentiality and Security Measures

LEARNERS: responses are anonymous at source. No name, employee number, email address,
IP address, device identifier, or any other identifying information is collected at any
point; there is no login and no cookie; and no submission can be linked to a person, to
another submission, or to anything done in the training software. Because no identifying
data exists, individual participants cannot be identified by the researcher, by their
employer, or by anyone else, and no step of pseudonymization is needed or possible.
Responses are stored in an encrypted, access-controlled database reachable only over
encrypted connections, with access restricted to the researcher by a credential that is
not shared with facilitators. Data are retained only for the duration of the research
and destroyed thereafter, per the study's data management plan.

PRACTITIONERS: interviews are confidential rather than anonymous. Recordings, where
made, are transcribed by the researcher personally, without the use of automated
transcription services; transcripts are pseudonymized at transcription; organizations
are not named; and quotations are checked with the speaker before use. Recordings are
deleted after transcription. Interview materials are stored encrypted and
password-protected, accessible to the researcher alone.

PUBLICATION: in any publication or presentation, no identifying information of any
participant or client organization is disclosed. Findings are reported in aggregate for
learners and under pseudonyms, with organizations described only by sector and size,
for practitioners.

## Data Sharing

No individual-level data is shared with any third party. Client organizations receive
at most group-level summaries in which no individual can be distinguished. Supervisory
review at Bocconi University is conducted on the same non-identifying material.
