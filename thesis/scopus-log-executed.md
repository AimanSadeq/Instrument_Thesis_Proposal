## Methodological notes and caveats

1. **Result counts are database state as of 17 August 2026.** Scopus indexes continuously, and "Article in Press" records are included. Re-running these queries on a later date will return higher counts. For a reproducible systematic review, record the exact date (done here) and, ideally, export the full record set.

2. **Scopus caps browsable results at 2,000.** For Queries 1, 3, 3b, 5 and 6 the results list header reads "showing results 1 through 10 of 2000 results" while the headline count (4,609 / 11,860 / 8,530 / 2,380 / 2,765) is the true total. Only the first 2,000 can be paged through or exported in one pass; larger sets must be sliced (e.g. by year) to export in full.

3. **The "Documents" tab was used throughout.** Preprints are counted separately by Scopus (e.g. 823 preprints for Query 1, 2,547 for Query 3b) and are excluded from the totals above.

4. **Relevance ranking in Scopus is not transparent.** It weights term frequency and field position, not citation impact — which is why Query 1's top hit ("Large Language Models and Computer Security") is topically off-target despite ranking first. For screening purposes, "Cited by (highest)" often surfaces more useful anchor papers.

5. **Diacritics.** In Query 2 the term "Gagné" was transmitted to the Scopus search endpoint HTML-entity-encoded (`Gagn&#233;`). The query as displayed back by Scopus reads "Gagné" correctly and the search executed, but if you need certainty on that term, re-run Query 2 with "Gagne" (unaccented) and compare the counts.

6. **Broad single terms inflate counts.** "simulation" (Q3b), "AI" (Q5, Q6), "TAM" (Q6) and "ROI" (Q5) are short or ambiguous strings that match large volumes of unrelated literature. See the per-query notes above.
